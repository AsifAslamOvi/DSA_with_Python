Bangladesh is a South Asian country located on the Bay of Bengal, bordered by India 
and Myanmar. It gained independence in 1971 and has a rich cultural heritage shaped by 
Bengali language, literature, music, and traditions. Dhaka, the capital city, is one of 
the most densely populated cities in the world and serves as the country’s political, 
economic, and cultural center.

The country is known for its fertile river deltas formed by the Ganges, Brahmaputra, 
and Meghna rivers. Agriculture plays an important role in the economy, while the 
ready-made garment industry is a major driver of exports. Bangladesh has made 
significant progress in education, healthcare, and poverty reduction over recent 
decades, and it continues to grow as a developing economy.

Bangladesh is also famous for natural and cultural landmarks such as the Sundarbans 
mangrove forest, home to the Royal Bengal Tiger, and Cox’s Bazar, which has one of the 
longest natural sea beaches in the world.
